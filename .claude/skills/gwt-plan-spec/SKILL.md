## When to use

Turn settled requirements into an implementation-ready plan and traceable tasks. Use lightweight planning for an approved task without a SPEC.

## Completion

Persist the required plan/tasks and any supporting artifacts the scope needs. Resolve critical ambiguity, respect active claims, and settle the planning lifecycle. CLEAR means ready for implementation, not implemented or delivered.
