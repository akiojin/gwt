# GWT Feedback

Usage: `/gwt:gwt-feedback [observed gwt problem]`

Load `.claude/skills/gwt-feedback/SKILL.md` and follow it. Collect the affected
operation, complete original refusal, reproduction steps or `unconfirmed`, and
any workaround. Submit through `feedback.create`, retain the durable receipt,
and continue the project's work while native user approval is pending.
