## When to use

Investigate unresolved design or scope questions and produce a decision-ready next action. Already settled work can proceed to planning or execution.

## Completion

Resolve consequential unknowns with evidence, select one primary execution owner, and record concrete artifact changes and next actions. Preserve discussion lifecycle and approval boundaries; do not turn a discussion into unauthorized implementation.
